Mission 6 · 47–55 min

🎤 Speak & Describe

Speaking: Choose something to describe

Try to speak for 45–90 seconds. Your teacher may ask follow-up questions.

Writing: Describe something you love

Write 4–6 sentences describing your favorite game, superhero, sport, or food. Tell us what it is, what it looks like, and why you like it.
